# UBC authorship

**UBC — Universal Bot Communication was created by Ryan Sloan and Dustin Keesbury.**

Ryan conceived UBC as an open communication layer for personal agents, business agents and owner-authorized devices. Dustin Keesbury co-originated the idea and helped shape its early direction. The project welcomes independent implementations and contributions.

UBC is free and open source under the MIT license. Creator attribution does not create a paid license, exclusive network, required Ghostlight account or restriction on commercial use. Preserve the copyright and license notice as required by the MIT license.

Contributors are credited through the repository history and release notes. Implementations should accurately identify their protocol version and avoid claiming endorsement or security certification that has not been granted.
